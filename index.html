How I Work

A simple, honest process.

How I approach any task — practice or real.

01

Ask, then investigate

Before touching any tool, I write down the actual question. What's broken, what's unclear, and what would "fixed" look like? No tool-first guessing.

02

Document the process

Every audit, keyword map, or fix list comes with a short writeup — the tools used, the data pulled, the reasoning, and screenshots. So you can follow my thinking.

03

Ask for feedback

I share practice work with mentors, communities, and peers for critique before claiming it's done. Learning fast means hearing what's wrong early.

FAQ

Common questions.

Straight answers — including the awkward ones.

You're a beginner — why should I hire you?

Because hiring juniors is a bet on trajectory, not track record. What I offer: a clean documented process, a willingness to ask questions, a fast learning pace (evidenced by the practice portfolio), and a low rate that reflects my level. If you want a cheap pair of hands to do junior SEO tasks under supervision, I'm a strong fit.

What can I realistically assign to you on day one?

Technical audits on small-to-medium sites, keyword research and intent clustering, on-page fix lists, GA4 + Search Console setup and reporting, and basic schema recommendations. I'm honest about what I don't know yet — advanced log-file analysis, enterprise-scale migration work, and complex link-building outreach are areas I'd want to learn alongside you, not run solo.

What access do you need to get started?

Read-only access to Google Search Console and Google Analytics 4 (or an editor role if you want me to make changes), plus site admin access if I'll be touching pages. I can work with whatever access level you're comfortable with — and I'm happy to sign an NDA before anything else.

What are your rates?

Entry-level rates that reflect my experience. I'm flexible between hourly, project-based, and monthly retainer — whatever fits your team's way of working. My priority in early roles is learning alongside good practitioners.

How do you report on progress?

Every task comes with a short writeup — what I did, what I found, what I recommend, and what I'd do next. You'll never get a black box. Monthly reports pull directly from GA4 and Search Console.

Are the practice projects really self-initiated?

Yes. Every audit, keyword map, and fix list on this site was self-initiated on a public website or a fictional scenario — no client paid for them. That's the point of a practice portfolio: real work, honest framing, nothing invented.
